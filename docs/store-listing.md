### 詳細說明 (Detailed Description)
**Blob Todo - 視覺化你的輕量任務管理**

每天面對落落長的代辦清單感到壓力山大嗎？Blob Todo 用一種全新、有機且具生命力的方式，顛覆傳統條列式的管理體驗。每當你開啟 Chrome 的新分頁，你不再看見冰冷的文字，而是一個個像水滴般柔軟、會互相推擠的「任務泡泡」。

✨ **核心特色：**
- **重要度視覺化**：越重要的任務泡泡越大，最緊急的事情總是第一眼抓住你的目光。
- **色彩提醒機制**：隨著截止日期逼近，泡泡的顏色會自然地從冷色系轉變成溫暖的急迫色。
- **物理碰撞體驗**：用滑鼠與泡泡互動，看著它們在畫面中流暢地互相擠壓、重排。
- **子任務無縫展開**：點擊大型任務，直接潛入它的子任務世界，保持焦點不分心。
- **極簡體驗無壓力**：點破完成的泡泡，享受它消失帶來的純粹成就感！

讓 Blob Todo 幫你找回工作的節奏與樂趣，把壓力轉換成在畫面中漂浮的輕盈泡泡！

## [English]

### Name
Blob Todo

### Short Description / Summary
Bring your tasks to life! Visualize what matters most with organic, physics-based bubbles on every new tab.

### Detailed Description
**Blob Todo - Visual, Organic Task Management**

Feeling overwhelmed by endless, boring to-do lists? Blob Todo completely reimagines task management with an organic, lively, and stress-free visual experience. Every time you open a new tab in Chrome, you won't see cold rows of text. Instead, you'll be greeted by soft, fluid "task bubbles" that gently bump and push against each other.

✨ **Core Features:**
- **Visual Prioritization**: The more important the task, the larger the bubble. Your most critical tasks will always catch your eye first.
- **Color-coded Urgency**: As deadlines approach, bubble colors naturally shift from cool, calm tones to warm, urgent colors.
- **Physics-Based Interaction**: Watch your tasks smoothly reposition, squeeze, and float around your screen.
- **Subtask Deep Dive**: Click on any large bubble to dive into its subtasks, keeping your focus sharp and distraction-free.
- **Stress-Free Satisfaction**: Mark a task as done and enjoy the satisfying animation of popping the bubble!

## [隱私權與權限說明 / Privacy & Permissions]

### 單一用途說明 (Single Purpose Statement)
**Blob Todo** 的唯一用途是為使用者提供一個視覺化、具備物理互動感的輕量任務管理介面，並將其整合於 Chrome 的「新分頁」中，幫助使用者直覺地掌握任務優先順序。

**Blob Todo**'s single purpose is to provide users with a visual, physics-based, lightweight task management interface integrated into the Chrome "New Tab" page, helping users intuitively prioritize their daily tasks.

### 權限要求理由 (Permission Justification)
- **storage**: 
  - **理由**：我們需要此權限來儲存使用者所建立的任務清單、子任務以及其相關的權重與截止日期。
  - **資料處理**：所有資料均儲存於使用者的本機瀏覽器中，本擴充功能不會將任何個人資料上傳至外部伺服器。
  
- **storage**: 
  - **Justification**: We require this permission to save and persist the task lists, subtasks, and their associated weights and deadlines created by the user.
  - **Data Handling**: All data is stored locally within the user's browser. This extension does not upload any personal data to external servers.
